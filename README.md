# 🔊 Loudillys - Volume & Speed Booster

Make quiet videos louder and control playback speed on any website.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔊 **Boost Volume up to 5x**: Make quiet videos louder than your computer's normal limit (from 1x up to 5x).
- ⚡ **Change Video Speed**: Speed up or slow down videos from 0.25x to 4x.
- 🛡️ **Smooth Playback**: If a video starts buffering or lagging when sped up, it automatically slows down so you don't get stuck.
- 🌐 **Works Everywhere**: Works on YouTube, online courses, news sites, and any web video.
- 🌍 **Multiple Languages**: Available in English, Portuguese, and Spanish (change it right from the popup).
- 🎛️ **Instant Control**: Just move the sliders and hear the difference immediately.

---

## 📥 How to Install

You can install this extension on **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, **Microsoft Edge**, or any Chromium browser in just a minute:

### 1. Download the Files

- Click the green **Code** button at the top of this GitHub page.
- Click **Download ZIP**.
- Unzip (extract) the downloaded folder somewhere easy to find (like your Desktop or Downloads folder).

*(If you use Git, you can clone the repository instead).*

### 2. Add it to Your Browser

1. In your browser's address bar, go to:
   - Chrome: `chrome://extensions`
   - Edge: `edge://extensions`
   - Brave: `brave://extensions`
2. Turn on the **Developer mode** switch (in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button (in the top-left corner).
4. Select the unzipped folder.

### 3. Pin it to Your Toolbar (Recommended)

Click the **puzzle icon (🧩)** in your browser toolbar, then click the **pin icon** next to **Loudillys** so it's always ready to use.

---

## 🎯 How to Use

1. Open any website with a video (like YouTube).
2. Click the **Loudillys icon** in your browser toolbar.
3. Move the sliders:
   - **Volume**: Slide right to make the video louder (up to 5x).
   - **Speed**: Slide to speed up or slow down the video.
4. That's it! The audio and speed change instantly.

---

## 💡 Good to Know

- **Current Video Only**: When you open the extension, it automatically shows the current volume and speed of the video playing on your screen.
- **Streaming Services**: On sites like Netflix or Disney+, the volume boost may not work due to browser copy-protection restrictions.
